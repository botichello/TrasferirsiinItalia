In **Venezia**, residency is handled by the **Anagrafe**, with EU-citizen matters
supported by a dedicated *Ufficio soggiorno cittadini comunitari*.

- **How to submit:** book a residence-change appointment online (the *Dime*
  appointments service) or via the unified call centre on **041041**; you can also
  use the national **ANPR** portal (SPID/CIE) for certificates.
- **Deadline:** make the residence declaration within 20 days of moving.
- **Documents (EU citizens):** valid passport/ID, codice fiscale, and proof of the
  basis for your stay (work, study + cover, or sufficient resources + cover).
