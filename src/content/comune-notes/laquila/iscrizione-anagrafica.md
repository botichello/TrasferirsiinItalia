In **L'Aquila**, the residency declaration goes through the **Ufficio Anagrafe**
(Via Roma 207/A — Palazzo Del Tosto), which accepts several channels alongside
the national **ANPR** portal.

- **How to submit:** online via **ANPR** with **SPID, CIE** or **CNS**; by plain
  email to *anagrafe@comune.laquila.it* (scanned form with handwritten signature
  plus ID copy); by PEC to *protocollo@comune.laquila.postecert.it*; by
  registered mail; or in person at the office.
- **Forms & documents:** the ministerial declaration form (downloadable from the
  comune's transparency portal), a photocopy of your ID, proof of title to the
  property — or the landlord's consent form if you rent — and a declaration of
  non-abusive occupation. EU citizens add the national-rule proof of the basis
  for the stay (work, study + cover, or sufficient resources + cover).
- **Timeline & checks:** the national two-working-day rule applies; if you hear
  nothing within **45 days** of the request, it counts as accepted
  (silence-assent), with address checks in the meantime.
- **Counter hours:** Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri 9:00–12:00, plus Tue/Wed 15:30–17:30 —
  counters close sharp at closing time regardless of the queue.
