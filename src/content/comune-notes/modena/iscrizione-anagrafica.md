In **Modena**, residency declarations are submitted **online** through the
comune's **Resiweb** portal (resiweb.comune.modena.it).

- **EU citizens:** attach the documentation in **Allegato B**; the Registry Office
  also issues the *attestazione di iscrizione anagrafica* and, after 5 years, the
  permanent-residence attestation.
- **Timeline:** the change is registered within **2 working days** of receipt; the
  process concludes within 45 days.
- **Documents (EU citizens):** valid passport/ID, codice fiscale, and proof of the
  basis for your stay (work, study + cover, or sufficient resources + cover).
