In **Lucca** (on ANPR since December 2017), the residency declaration is filed
**remotely**: in-person reception by appointment is currently suspended.

- **How to submit:** online via the comune's *Sportello Telematico* with **SPID,
  CIE or CNS**; or send the signed form with scanned (not photographed)
  attachments to **PEC** `comune.lucca@postacert.toscana.it` or email
  `protocollo@comune.lucca.it`; or by registered mail to *Comune di Lucca —
  Anagrafe, Via S. Giustina 6*. The Anagrafe sits under **U.O. 3.3 — Servizi
  Demografici** (Via S. Paolino 140).
- **Timeline:** effects run from the declaration date; registration within two
  working days, with Polizia Municipale address checks within 45 days
  (silence = approval). Filing is free.
- **Documents (EU citizens):** valid ID/passport, codice fiscale, proof of your
  legal occupancy of the home (registered lease, deed, or owner's declaration —
  unregistered contracts are rejected), and proof of the basis of stay (work,
  study + cover, or resources + cover) per the comune's *Allegato B*; the
  attestazione request takes a **€16 marca da bollo**.
