In **Torino**, EU citizens staying more than three months register in the
resident population register through the **Anagrafe**.

- **How to submit:** in person at a district Anagrafe office, **online via the
  TorinoFacile portal** (SPID/CIE), through an authorised CAF, or by registered mail
  or PEC.
- **Deadline:** submit the residence declaration to the Anagrafe **within 20 days**
  of moving.
- **Offices / appointments:** the central Anagrafe is at **Via della Consolata 23**,
  with branch offices around the city; an **appointment is required** for
  residence changes coming from outside Torino or from abroad.
- **Attestation:** after registering, you can request the *attestazione di
  iscrizione anagrafica* — issued **within 30 days**, with a **€16 marca da bollo**.
- **Documents (EU citizens):** valid passport/ID, codice fiscale, and proof of the
  basis for your stay (work, study + cover, or sufficient resources + cover).
