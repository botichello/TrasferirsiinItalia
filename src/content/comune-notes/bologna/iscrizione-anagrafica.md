In **Bologna**, EU citizens register through the comune's anagrafe, using the
registry declaration on the Ministry of the Interior forms.

- **Appointments:** you can **book an appointment online** with the comune's
  anagrafe / *uffici di prossimità* for in-person registration.
- **Timeline:** registration at the declared address generally happens **within 2
  working days** of the declaration; the comune carries out address checks **within
  45 days**.
- **Documents (EU citizens):** valid passport/ID, codice fiscale, and proof of the
  basis for your stay — being a worker or having sufficient resources, plus health
  insurance covering all risks.
