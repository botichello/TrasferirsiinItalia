In **Ferrara**, the change-of-residence declaration is filed **online**: through
the comune's *Sportello Telematico* (login with **SPID, CIE** or **CNS**) or
directly on the national **ANPR** portal. Filing is free.

- **Offices:** the *Ufficio Anagrafe* sits at **via F. Beretta 19**, with the
  *Sportello Centrale Anagrafe* (SCA) at **via F. Beretta 1** plus decentralised
  desks. In-person registry business requires an **appointment** booked on the
  comune's digital-services portal; info line **800 532 532**, email
  `ssdd@comune.fe.it`, PEC `comune.ferrara@cert.comune.fe.it`.
- **Timeline:** declare within **20 days** of moving; residency runs from the
  submission date and is recorded within **two working days**. The comune then
  has **45 days** to check the address — no news means silent approval.
- **Attachments:** the declaration form (signed by all adult household members),
  IDs of everyone moving, the owner's declaration that the dwelling is available
  (with their ID), and the assent of the existing family-sheet holder if you
  join an established household.
- **EU citizens:** after three months you can also request the *attestazione di
  iscrizione anagrafica* online, attaching proof of your basis of stay (work,
  study or resources + health cover); a €16 *marca da bollo* may apply.
