In **Taranto**, the residency declaration is filed **online** through the
comune's *Sportello Telematico Polifunzionale*; the practice is handled by the
**Ufficio cambio residenza** (PEC: `anagrafe.comunetaranto@pec.rupar.puglia.it`,
switchboard 099 4581111).

- **How to submit:** authenticate with **SPID, CIE or CNS** and file the
  change-of-residence declaration; if a whole household moves, all adult
  members sign and any one of them can submit. Filing is free.
- **Timeline:** declare within **20 days** of moving; registration takes effect
  from the declaration date and is recorded within **two working days**. The
  comune then has **45 days** for checks — no news means approval
  (*silenzio-assenso*).
- **Documents:** IDs of everyone moving, codice fiscale, your title to occupy
  the dwelling (lease, deed, comodato), plus the owner's availability
  declaration with a copy of their ID. EU citizens also attach the national
  proof-of-stay set (work, study or sufficient resources + health cover).
- **Local quirks:** put your surname on the **doorbell and mailbox** — the
  Polizia Locale verifies the address in person — and regularise your **TARI**
  (waste tax) position at the same time. Once in ANPR, your address updates
  automatically with Motorizzazione, INPS and Agenzia delle Entrate.
