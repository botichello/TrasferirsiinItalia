> **Draft — verify with your local ATS/ASST before relying on it.**

In **Lombardia**, the health system is organised around **ATS** (Agenzie di
Tutela della Salute) and **ASST** (Aziende Socio-Sanitarie Territoriali).
Enrolment in the SSN and choosing a *medico di base* is handled through the ATS
covering your registered address (in Milano, ATS Città Metropolitana di Milano).

- **Where to enrol:** the ATS for your registered address; use the Regione
  Lombardia health pages to identify it and its counters.
- **Choosing a doctor:** select a *medico di base* from those available in your
  ATS area.
- **Economically inactive EU citizens:** confirm with your ATS whether voluntary
  enrolment and the related annual contribution apply — set regionally, so the
  amount differs from other regions.
