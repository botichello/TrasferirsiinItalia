In **Catanzaro**, the residency declaration is still **form-based**: you download
the ministerial forms from the comune's site and file them through one of several
channels.

- **How to submit:** in person at the *protocollo* and *anagrafe* counters
  (Ufficio Anagrafe, via Jannoni 68), by registered mail, by email
  (`servizi.demografici@comune.catanzaro.it`), by PEC
  (`servizidemografici@certificata.comune.catanzaro.it`), or online via the
  national **ANPR** portal with **SPID/CIE**.
- **Deadline & timeline:** declare within **20 days** of the move; the comune
  registers it within two working days, then verifies requirements within 45
  days (no news within that window means it stands).
- **Documents (EU citizens):** attach the paperwork listed in the comune's
  **Allegato B**, plus the **modello IUC** and proof you legitimately occupy the
  dwelling (cadastral record if owner, registered lease/comodato, or the current
  occupant's — and owner's — signed consent if joining an existing household).
  EU citizens arriving from another Italian comune don't need to re-document the
  legal basis of stay.
- **Cost:** free. Responsible office: *Settore Servizi Demografici, Statistica e
  Sport* (tel. +39 0961 8811).
