In **Pisa**, foreign citizens registering for the first time use the residence
declaration; EU citizens staying over 3 months register under Legislative Decree
30/2007.

- **How to submit:** via the national **ANPR** portal, by email
  (cambiresidenza@comune.pisa.it) or PEC (comune.pisa.anagrafe@pec.it), or in
  person at the central office (Via C. Battisti 53) **by appointment**.
- **Timeline:** the anagrafe officer registers the declaration within **2 working
  days**; requirements are checked within 45 days.
- **Documents (EU citizens):** valid passport/ID, codice fiscale, and proof of the
  basis for your stay (work, study + cover, or sufficient resources + cover).
