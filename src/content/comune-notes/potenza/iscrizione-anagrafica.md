In **Potenza**, the comune points residents to the national **ANPR** portal for the
online residence declaration, alongside traditional local channels.

- **Online:** the comune's "Cambio di residenza e abitazione online" service links
  straight to ANPR — log in with **SPID, CIE** or **CNS** and submit the declaration
  for yourself and your household. ANPR also has a dedicated **EU-citizen transfer
  service** for first registration from abroad (no digital identity required to start).
- **In person:** the *sportello anagrafico* is on **Via Nazario Sauro** (Mobility
  Center). Demographic offices receive the public **without appointment** — Mon,
  Wed, Fri mornings 8:30–11:30; Tue and Thu afternoons only, 15:30–17:30.
- **Other channels:** the comune also accepts the ministerial declaration form by
  registered mail, by ordinary email (**cambioresidenza@comune.potenza.it**, with
  signed scan + ID copy) or by PEC (**protocollo@pec.comune.potenza.it**).
- **Timeline:** the national rule applies — registration within **two working days**
  of submission, effective from the declaration date, with address checks (up to
  45 days) afterwards.
- **Documents (EU citizens):** valid ID/passport, codice fiscale, and proof of the
  basis of stay (work, or study/self-sufficiency plus health cover) per the
  ministerial *allegato B* checklist.
