> **Draft — verify with ASReM before relying on it.**

In **Molise**, the health service is delivered through a **single** regional
authority, **ASReM** (Azienda Sanitaria Regionale del Molise), which covers the
whole region through its socio-health districts (in Campobasso, the *Distretto
Socio-Sanitario di Campobasso*).

- **Where to enrol:** the ASReM district counter (*sportello*) for your registered
  address.
- **Choosing a doctor:** select a *medico di base* from those available in your
  district.
- **Economically inactive EU citizens:** confirm with ASReM whether voluntary
  enrolment and the related annual contribution apply — this is set regionally,
  so the amount differs from other regions.
