In **Sassari**, residency is handled by the registry office (*Anagrafe
programmazione e staff*) via the **Punto Città** desk, **Via Giovanni Maria
Angioy 15**.

- **How to submit:** online through the national **ANPR** portal with **SPID,
  CIE or CNS**, or through Punto Città (tel. +39 079 279886, email
  `puntocitta@comune.sassari.it`, PEC `protocollo@pec.comune.sassari.it`).
  Declare the move on the ministerial form **within 20 days**. The foreigners'
  desk also offers **video-call appointments** (no software needed).
- **Timeline:** the declaration is recorded within **two working days**, the
  municipal police address check follows within 30 days, and confirmation or
  refusal comes within 45 days.
- **Documents (EU citizens):** passport/national ID, codice fiscale, plus proof
  of your basis for staying — e.g. hiring notice or INPS receipts/payslips for
  employees, or partita IVA + chamber-of-commerce registration if
  self-employed. After three months in Italy you must register and can request
  the *attestazione di iscrizione anagrafica* (two €16 revenue stamps).
- **Quirk:** after registering, Sassari asks you to file the **TARI** (waste
  tax) declaration for your new home.
