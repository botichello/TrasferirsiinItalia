In **Lecce**, EU citizens use the *"Iscrizione anagrafica comunitari"* service,
with the form downloadable from the Registry Office page.

- **How to submit:** by registered mail to via Rubichi 16; or electronically — by
  PEC to protocollo@pec.comune.lecce.it (from a PEC address only), or a scanned,
  signed declaration with ID copy by ordinary email to anagrafe@pec.comune.lecce.it.
- **Timeline:** completed within **2 working days**; if you receive no
  missing-requirements notice within 45 days, registration is confirmed.
- **Attestation:** EU citizens registered under 5 years get an attestation of
  regular residence; after 5 years of continuous legal residence, the permanent
  one. Office: Ufficio Anagrafe, viale Aldo Moro 36.
