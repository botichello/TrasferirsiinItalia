> **Draft — verify with your local ASL before relying on it.**

In **Lazio**, the health service is delivered through the regional system
coordinated via the **Salute Lazio** portal, with enrolment handled by the local
**ASL** for the area where you are registered as resident (in Roma, this is one
of the ASL Roma authorities, depending on your address).

- **Where to enrol:** the ASL covering your registered address. Use the Salute
  Lazio portal to find your ASL and its *sportello* (counter).
- **Choosing a doctor:** you select a *medico di base* from those available in
  your ASL district.
- **Economically inactive EU citizens:** if you are not enrolling on the basis of
  work, confirm with your ASL whether voluntary enrolment (and its annual
  contribution) applies to your situation — this is set regionally and the amount
  is not uniform across Italy.
