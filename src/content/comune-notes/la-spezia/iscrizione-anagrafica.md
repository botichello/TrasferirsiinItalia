In **La Spezia**, the registry office (*Anagrafe – Demografia e statistica*) is
in the Palazzo Civico, **Piazza Europa 1, 19124** — desks open Mon–Fri
08:00–12:00 and Sat 08:00–11:15.

- **Online:** the comune routes residence declarations through the national
  **ANPR** portal — log in with **SPID, CIE, CNS or eIDAS** (an EU digital
  identity works) and start a *dichiarazione di trasferimento*. There is a
  dedicated flow for EU citizens: it lets you upload documents or pull them
  from another EU administration via **OOTS**, and optionally leave a phone
  number and time slot for the address check.
- **In person:** book on the comune's website, then bring your ID and the
  ministerial declaration form signed by every adult in the household (with
  copies of their IDs), the exact address including street number and
  *interno*, the housing-title section filled in, and your driving licence and
  vehicle details to declare on the same form. Free of charge.
- **Documents (EU citizens, stays over 3 months):** passport/ID, codice
  fiscale if you have one, property details or lease + the owner's ID, plus
  proof of your basis of stay — work, or study/no work with a
  sufficient-resources self-declaration and health insurance valid at least a
  year (the EHIC card is not accepted). Non-EU family members of an EU citizen
  use the same service.
- **Checks and timing:** declare within 20 days of moving (national rule; the
  comune notes EU citizens face no fine for registering late); the local
  police verify the address within **45 days**, after which the registration
  counts as perfected if you hear nothing.
- **Contacts:** PEC demografici.laspezia@legalmail.it · 0187 727 270 / 727 706.
