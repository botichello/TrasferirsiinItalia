The **codice fiscale** is your Italian tax identification number: a 16-character
alphanumeric code derived from your name, date and place of birth, and sex. It is
the single most foundational thing you will obtain when settling in Italy —
almost nothing else can be done without it.

## Why you need it first

You will be asked for a codice fiscale to:

- sign an employment or self-employment contract;
- [open an Italian bank account](/banking);
- sign a residential lease;
- register with the national health service (SSN);
- set up utilities, a mobile contract, and most other services.

Because so many later steps depend on it, getting the codice fiscale is **Step 1**
of the residency journey.

## Who issues it

The codice fiscale is issued by the **Agenzia delle Entrate** (the Italian Revenue
Agency), free of charge. As an EU/EEA citizen you have two main routes:

1. **Before you arrive** — request it through an Italian consulate in your country
   of residence. This is often the fastest path overall, because you arrive ready
   to act.
2. **After you arrive** — apply in person at any Agenzia delle Entrate office.

## How to apply in person

1. Bring a valid passport or national ID card.
2. Complete the request form for individuals (**modello AA4/8**). Staff at the
   office can help if you have not filled it in advance.
3. Submit it at the counter. In most cases the codice fiscale is issued
   **immediately**, on a printed certificate (*certificato di attribuzione*).

> The plastic health card (*tessera sanitaria*) that also shows your codice
> fiscale is a separate, later step — you receive it after enrolling in the
> national health service. See Step 3.

## Common pitfalls

- **Treating it as a residence permit.** It is not. EU citizens establish their
  right to stay by registering residency at the comune (Step 2), not by holding a
  permesso di soggiorno.
- **Name mismatches.** The code is generated from your legal name exactly as it
  appears on your ID. Inconsistencies between documents (e.g. middle names) can
  cause problems later — make sure the details match.
- **Losing the certificate.** Keep the issued certificate safe; you will be asked
  to quote the code constantly in your first months.

## Next step

With your codice fiscale in hand, you can register your residency at your local
comune — which, for stays over three months, is both your legal obligation and
the gateway to the health service.
