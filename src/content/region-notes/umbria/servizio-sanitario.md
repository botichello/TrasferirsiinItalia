> **Draft — verify with your local USL before relying on it.**

In **Umbria**, the health service is delivered through two **USL** (Aziende
Unità Sanitarie Locali): **USL Umbria 1** (Perugia area) and **USL Umbria 2**
(Terni/southern area). In Perugia you enrol with **USL Umbria 1**.

- **Where to enrol:** the USL covering your registered address; find its counters
  (*sportelli*) via the USL site or the Regione Umbria health pages.
- **Choosing a doctor:** select a *medico di base* from those available in your
  USL district.
- **Economically inactive EU citizens:** confirm with your USL whether voluntary
  enrolment and the related annual contribution apply — this is set regionally,
  so the amount differs from other regions.
