In **Bergamo**, residency registration is largely **online** through the comune's
*Sportello Telematico* and the national **ANPR** portal.

- **How to submit:** authenticate with **SPID, CIE** or **CNS** and submit the
  change-of-residence declaration for yourself and your household online; you can
  also consult your registry data and produce certificates without going to an
  office.
- **Timeline:** registration follows the national rule (completed within two
  working days), with address checks afterwards.
- **Documents (EU citizens):** valid passport/ID, codice fiscale, and proof of the
  basis for your stay (work, study + cover, or sufficient resources + cover).
