In **Cagliari**, residency uses the unified residence-declaration form (covering
arrival from another comune or from abroad). EU citizens attach the documents in
the form's **Allegato B**.

- **How to submit:** by PEC or email (cambioresidenza@comune.cagliari.legalmail.it),
  registered mail or fax to the Anagrafe (Piazza Alcide de Gasperi 2), or online via
  the national **ANPR** portal (SPID/CIE). The counter handles information only, not
  submissions.
- **Timeline:** registration is completed within **2 working days**, effective from
  the declaration date; the registry officer may verify residence within 45 days.
- **Documents (EU citizens):** valid passport/ID, codice fiscale, and proof of the
  basis for your stay (work, study + cover, or sufficient resources + cover).
