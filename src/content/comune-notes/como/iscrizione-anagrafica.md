In **Como**, the residency declaration goes to the Anagrafe (Settore *Servizi
Delegati*) at **Palazzo Cernezzi, Via Bertinelli, 22100 Como**, and can be
submitted through several channels.

- **How to submit:** online via the national **ANPR** portal (SPID/CIE/CNS); by
  plain email to **anagrafe.iscrizioni@comune.como.it** (scanned signed form +
  ID copy) or PEC to **anagrafe.iscrizioni@comune.pec.como.it**; by registered
  mail to Via Vittorio Emanuele II 97; or at the desk **without appointment**,
  Mon–Fri 8:30–12:30 (phone info 13:00–14:00: 031.252374 / 031.252218).
- **Local quirk:** you **must** attach proof of your right to occupy the home
  (lease, deed, comodato…) or complete the substitute declaration in the form —
  otherwise the declaration is **not accepted**. At the desk, bring documents
  in original plus photocopy.
- **Timeline:** registered within **2 working days**; address and requirement
  checks follow within 45 days.
- **EU citizens:** after 3 months you must register; bring passport/ID plus the
  circolare 9/2012 Allegato B documents (work, or study/resources + health
  insurance — EHIC not accepted). The *attestazione di regolarità di soggiorno*
  is issued on request once registered.
