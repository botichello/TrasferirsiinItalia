In **Cosenza**, residency runs through the **Ufficio Anagrafe** at
**Piazza Giacomo Mancini 33** — with unusually tight desk hours.

- **Desk hours:** the anagrafe/stato civile counters are open **only Monday and
  Thursday, 9:00–12:00**; appointments can be booked through the comune's
  services portal (switchboard **+39 0984 8131**).
- **Paper route:** download the **"Dichiarazione di residenza — Allegato 1"**
  form plus the transmission cover sheet from the comune site, and send them
  with your documents via PEC to **protocollo@pec.comune.cosenza.it** or hand
  them in at the desk.
- **Online (ANPR):** submit the change-of-residence declaration on the national
  ANPR portal with **SPID/CIE**; EU citizens (and their non-EU family) staying
  over three months can file through ANPR's dedicated *servizi per cittadini
  europei*, free of charge, with the declaration then handled by Cosenza's
  registry officers.
- **Documents (EU citizens):** valid ID/passport, codice fiscale (if held),
  property or lease details for the address, plus proof of your basis of stay —
  work, or study/self-sufficiency with sufficient resources and health
  insurance valid at least one year (the EHIC card is not accepted).
- **After five years:** the Anagrafe also issues the *attestazione di soggiorno
  permanente* for EU citizens, on a downloadable request form.
