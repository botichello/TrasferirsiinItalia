In **Trieste**, registration uses the Ministry of the Interior forms (downloadable
from the comune site or available at the Registry / Civic Centers).

- **How to submit:** at the counter (passo Costanzi 2, ground floor), by email
  (variazioneres@comune.trieste.it), by PEC (comune.trieste@certgov.fvg.it), or by
  registered mail.
- **EU citizens:** declare residing in Trieste for at least three months and prove
  your title to the property (lease or ownership); if you lack one, the owner
  completes the *dichPROPRIETARIO* form.
- **Timeline:** registration is completed within **2 working days** of the
  declaration.
- **Documents (EU citizens):** valid passport/ID, codice fiscale, and proof of the
  basis for your stay (work, study + cover, or sufficient resources + cover).
