In **Catania**, residency registration is run by the **Servizi Demografici**, with
the central Anagrafe at Via Alessandro La Marmora 23 and services also delivered
through the city's six *Municipi* (district offices).

- **How to submit:** forms are available on the Comune di Catania anagrafe pages;
  submit at the central office or your *Municipio* **by appointment**.
- **Office:** Direzione Servizi Demografici, Via Alessandro La Marmora 23
  (PEC: servizidemografici.catania@pec.it).
- **Documents (EU citizens):** valid passport/ID, codice fiscale, and proof of the
  basis for your stay (work, study + cover, or sufficient resources + cover).
