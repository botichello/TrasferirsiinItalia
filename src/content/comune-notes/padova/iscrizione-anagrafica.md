In **Padova**, EU citizens arriving from abroad use the dedicated
*"Iscrizione anagrafica per cittadini dell'Unione Europea"* service.

- **How to submit:** online via the national **ANPR** portal; or via the
  Immigration Office (Ufficio Immigrazioni), Piazza dei Signori 23 — **by
  appointment** — or by registered mail / PEC (immigrazione@pec.comune.padova.it).
  The service is free.
- **Timeline:** declare within 20 days of moving; the request is deemed accepted
  if you receive no negative communication within 45 days.
- **Attestation:** EU citizens can obtain the *attestazione di iscrizione
  anagrafica* (and, after 5 years, the *attestato di soggiorno permanente*).
- **Documents (EU citizens):** valid passport/ID, codice fiscale, and proof of the
  basis for your stay (work, study + cover, or sufficient resources + cover).
