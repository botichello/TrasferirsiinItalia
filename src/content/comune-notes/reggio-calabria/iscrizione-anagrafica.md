In **Reggio Calabria**, residency is handled by the **Servizi Demografici** at
**Via Nicola Calipari 2/N, 89123 Reggio Calabria**, with a dedicated portal at
*servizidemografici.reggiocal.it*.

- **How to submit:** the comune recommends the national **ANPR** portal
  (*anagrafenazionale.interno.it*, SPID/CIE login); alternatively by **PEC** to
  `protocollo@pec.reggiocal.it`, by registered mail, or **in person** at the
  Via Calipari desks or at the *circoscrizioni* (district offices) — Mon–Fri
  8:45–12:00, Thursday also 15:00–16:45.
- **Form:** the *dichiarazione di residenza* PDF is downloadable from the
  service page, together with **Allegato B** listing the documents EU citizens
  must attach.
- **Timeline:** registration within **two working days** (national rule), but
  residency takes effect from the day you file; the *vigili urbani* (municipal
  police) then verify the address, and the previous comune deregisters you.
- **Documents (EU citizens):** valid ID/passport for every family member moving,
  plus proof for the basis of stay per Allegato B (work, study or sufficient
  resources + health cover). Filing is free.
