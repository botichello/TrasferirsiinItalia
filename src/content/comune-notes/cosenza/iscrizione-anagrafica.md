In **Cosenza**, residency is handled by the anagrafe of the comune's
demographic services (Settore 2), based at **Piazza Giacomo Mancini 33**. The
comune wants declarations sent **remotely**: going to the office in person is
for when you genuinely cannot use the other channels.

- **How to file:** download the **"Dichiarazione di residenza — Allegato 1"**
  form and the cover letter (*lettera di trasmissione*) from the comune site.
  Send them with your documents by email, by PEC to
  **protocollo@pec.comune.cosenza.it**, or by registered post with return
  receipt. Any extra documents the office asks for go the same way.
- **Contacts:** demographic services **+39 0984 813901**; switchboard
  **+39 0984 8131**. If you do need the desk, book through the site's
  *Prenotazione appuntamento* link.
- **Online (ANPR):** submit the change-of-residence declaration on the national
  ANPR portal with **SPID/CIE**; EU citizens (and their non-EU family) staying
  over three months can file through ANPR's dedicated *servizi per cittadini
  europei*, free of charge, with the declaration then handled by Cosenza's
  registry officers.
- **Documents (EU citizens):** valid ID/passport, codice fiscale (if held),
  property or lease details for the address, plus proof of your basis of stay —
  work, or study/self-sufficiency with sufficient resources and health
  insurance.
- **After five years:** the comune also issues the *attestazione di soggiorno
  permanente* for EU citizens (art. 14 D.Lgs. 30/2007), on a downloadable
  request form.
