In **Siena**, the residency declaration goes to the **Ufficio Anagrafe** within
20 days of moving; the preferred route is **online via the national ANPR portal**.

- **How to submit:** online through ANPR with **SPID, CIE or CNS**; otherwise
  download the comune's forms (*Allegato 1* plus occupancy-title declaration)
  and send them by email (`ufficio.protocollo@comune.siena.it`), PEC
  (`comune.siena@postacert.toscana.it`), or registered post to *Ufficio
  Anagrafe, Il Campo 1, 53100 Siena*. Or go in person to the **Servizio
  Demografico** desks at **via Salicotto 6** (walk-in Mon/Wed/Fri; Tue/Thu by
  appointment, tel. 0577 292200). Free of charge.
- **Timeline:** registered within **two working days**, effective from the
  submission date, with address checks in the following 45 days.
- **Documents (EU citizens, Allegato B):** ID/passport valid for travel, plus
  proof of work (employed or self-employed) *or* a declaration of sufficient
  resources with health insurance valid at least one year.
- **Local quirks:** the form must state your apartment's internal number
  (*numero interno*) where one exists, and registering triggers a **TARI**
  (waste-tax) declaration to SI.GE.RI.CO.
