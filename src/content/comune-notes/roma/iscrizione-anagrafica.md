In **Roma**, residency is registered at the **Ufficio Anagrafico of your Municipio**
(Roma is divided into 15 Municipi; you use the one for your address), not a single
central office.

- **How to submit:** the residence declaration, with all documents attached, is
  sent by **PEC** or registered mail, handed in at the *protocollo*, or made in
  person at the counter **by appointment only**.
- **Documents (EU citizens):** valid passport or ID, codice fiscale, proof of the
  basis for your stay (work, study + cover, or sufficient resources + cover), and
  civil-status documents not shown by your passport.
- **Attestation:** once registered, EU citizens can request the *attestato di
  soggiorno* / *attestazione di iscrizione anagrafica*.
