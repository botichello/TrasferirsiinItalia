In **Firenze**, EU citizens use the **"Residenza cittadino comunitario"** service.

- **How to submit:** the residence declaration can be sent to
  **iscrizioni.anagrafe@pec.comune.fi.it** — by PEC, or by ordinary email if
  signed and scanned with a copy of ID (no PEC account needed) — or by registered
  mail to the Direzione Servizi Territoriali, Palazzo Vecchio.
- **Documents (EU citizens):** valid passport, national ID or Italian ID for the
  applicant and each family member, plus a **health insurance** document for each
  person transferring residence.
- **Effect:** registration takes effect from the date the Comune receives the
  documentation. EU residency matters are supported by the Immigration Office at
  Villa Pallini, Via Baracca.
