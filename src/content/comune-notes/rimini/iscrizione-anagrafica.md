In **Rimini**, the change of residence must be communicated within **20 days** of
moving; EU citizens attach the documentation in **Allegato B**.

- **How to submit:** online via web or mobile with **SPID/CIE**, in person at the
  Registry Office (Via Caduti di Marzabotto 25), or by registered mail / email
  (anagrafe@pec.comune.rimini.it, from any address).
- **Contact:** 0541 704777, Mon–Fri 12:00–13:15.
- **Documents (EU citizens):** valid passport/ID, codice fiscale, and proof of the
  basis for your stay (work, study + cover, or sufficient resources + cover).
