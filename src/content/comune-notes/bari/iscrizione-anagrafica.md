In **Bari**, residency is handled by the **Servizi Demografici** (iscrizioni
anagrafiche e cambi d'indirizzo).

- **How to submit:** the residence declaration uses the national ministerial form;
  EU citizens attach the documents listed in its **Allegato B**. Requests can be
  sent to the anagrafe (PEC: anagrafe.comunebari@pec.rupar.puglia.it) and are
  processed within 30 days when sent by mail.
- **Attestation:** the *attestazione di iscrizione anagrafica* ("attestation of
  regular stay") is issued to EU citizens on request, on completing registration.
- **Documents (EU citizens):** valid passport/ID, codice fiscale, and proof of the
  basis for your stay (work, study + cover, or sufficient resources + cover).
