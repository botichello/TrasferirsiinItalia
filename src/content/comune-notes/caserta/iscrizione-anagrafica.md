In **Caserta**, residency is handled by the **Anagrafe** office (Servizi
demografici e Statistica) of the comune, based at Piazza Vanvitelli, 81100
Caserta — and the counter works **by booking only**.

- **Booking (the local quirk):** you reserve your slot by **SMS to
  333 61 85 367**, in the format `PRENOTA <codice fiscale> <office code>`;
  the code for *iscrizioni anagrafiche / variazioni di indirizzo* is **006**.
  Before booking, the comune suggests asking questions at
  **informazioni.anagrafe@comune.caserta.it** or **0823 273919**.
- **Opening hours:** Monday, Wednesday and Friday 9:00–12:00; Tuesday and
  Thursday 15:00–17:00. The office PEC is
  **postacertificata@pec.comune.caserta.it**.
- **Online via ANPR:** Caserta has migrated to the national registry, so if
  you are already registered in ANPR you can send the **change-of-residence
  declaration** online with SPID/CIE, and download certificates and your
  *visura anagrafica* without visiting the office.
- **EU citizens:** the same Anagrafe office handles registrations of EU
  nationals and issues the residence document for EU citizens (*carta di
  soggiorno cittadini comunitari*), so first-time registrations from abroad
  go through the booked counter rather than the ANPR portal.
