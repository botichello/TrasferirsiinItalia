In **Messina**, residency declarations are submitted through the comune's
**Sportello Telematico** (online desk) or at the city *municipalità*.

- **EU citizens:** staying over three months, apply for *iscrizione anagrafica*;
  once registered you can request the *attestazione di iscrizione anagrafica*,
  which counts as your residence title.
- **Timeline:** registration takes effect from the declaration date and is
  recorded within **2 working days**; requirements and the actual move are
  verified within 45 days.
- **Documents (EU citizens):** valid passport/ID, codice fiscale, and proof of the
  basis for your stay (work, study + cover, or sufficient resources + cover).
