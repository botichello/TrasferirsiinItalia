In **Palermo**, EU citizens register through the *"Iscrizione anagrafica per
cittadini europei"* service.

- **How to submit:** online via the national **ANPR** portal (logging in with
  **eIDAS / SPID / CIE**), or in person at the **Servizio Anagrafe** counter
  (Viale Lazio 119) **by appointment**.
- **Timeline:** the residence declaration should be made within 20 days; the
  comune processes it within about 30 days and notifies you by email.
- **Attestation:** once registered, EU citizens can request the *attestazione di
  iscrizione anagrafica*.
- **Documents (EU citizens):** valid passport/ID, codice fiscale, and proof of the
  basis for your stay (work, study + cover, or sufficient resources + cover).
