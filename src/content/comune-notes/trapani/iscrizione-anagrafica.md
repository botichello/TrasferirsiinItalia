In **Trapani**, the residency declaration goes through the national **ANPR**
portal (SPID or CIE) or on the ministerial paper form via several local channels.

- **How to submit:** online through ANPR ("Richiedi online" on the comune site);
  in person at the **ufficio protocollo generale**, ground floor of Palazzo
  Comunale in Piazza Municipio (or, for the online service assisted at a desk,
  the Borgo Madonna branch in Via G. Polizzi); or by **PEC**
  (`anagrafe@pec.comune.trapani.it`) / email (`anagrafe@comune.trapani.it`) with
  a digital signature or a scanned signed form plus ID.
- **Office:** Anagrafe e stato civile, Largo San Francesco di Paola 10, 91100
  Trapani. The service is free.
- **Documents (EU citizens):** the "allegato B" documentation set (ID/passport,
  proof of work, study or resources per the national rules), plus foreign
  civil-status certificates translated and legalised if registering family ties.
- **Local quirks:** attach photocopies of driving licences and vehicle
  registration documents for everyone moving, proof of your title to occupy the
  dwelling (owner's consent declaration if you are not the contract holder), and
  the **TARI waste-tax declaration**, which Trapani requires alongside the
  residency form. Checks at the declared address run within **45 days**; silence
  after that means the registration is confirmed.
