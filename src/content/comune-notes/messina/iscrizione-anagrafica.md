In **Messina**, you declare residency **online** with SPID, CIE or CNS through
the comune's services portal and its **Sportello Telematico**
(sportellotelematico.comune.messina.it), or **at the desk** by booking an
appointment. In November 2026 the comune's service pages marked the online
channel as temporarily unavailable, so book the desk if it is still down.
Switchboard **+39 090 7721**; PEC **protocollo@pec.comune.messina.it**.

- **EU citizens:** staying over three months, you can also file online on the
  national **ANPR** portal's service for European citizens ("Voglio iniziare ora
  una dichiarazione di trasferimento"). Registration is free.
- **Timeline:** registration takes effect from the declaration date and is
  recorded within **2 working days**; requirements and the actual move are
  verified within 45 days.
- **Documents (EU citizens):** valid passport/ID, codice fiscale if you have one,
  and the cadastral details of the home (or the lease and the owner's ID). Add
  proof of work; or, if you study or are self-sufficient, a self-declaration of
  sufficient resources and health insurance valid for at least a year — the
  EHIC is not accepted.
