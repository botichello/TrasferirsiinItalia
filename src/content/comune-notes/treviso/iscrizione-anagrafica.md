In **Treviso**, residency is handled by the **Anagrafe**, with an online
change-of-residence service via the regional *myPortal* platform.

- **At the counter:** bring a valid ID, and documentation proving your right to
  the property (ownership deed or rental contract, original or certified copy)
  under Law Decree 47/2014 — or a substitute declaration.
- **EU citizens:** registration follows the national EU-citizen procedure; bring
  proof of the basis for your stay.
- **Documents (EU citizens):** valid passport/ID, codice fiscale, and proof of the
  basis for your stay (work, study + cover, or sufficient resources + cover).
