In **Verona**, residency is handled by the **Ufficio Anagrafe** via the
*Dichiarazione di residenza / Cambio abitazione* service.

- **How to submit:** online through the national **ANPR** portal
  (SPID/CIE/CNS/eIDAS); EU citizens also complete the *dichiarazione risorse
  economiche* module and attach the EU-citizen documentation. Specific cases can
  be booked by email (iscrizionianagrafiche@comune.verona.it) or phone
  (045 8077822, Mon–Fri 12:00–13:00).
- **Timeline:** the comune verifies the documents and residence requirements
  within 45 days of submission.
- **Documents (EU citizens):** valid passport/ID, codice fiscale, and proof of the
  basis for your stay (work, study + cover, or sufficient resources + cover).
