**Pescara** has joined the Interior Ministry's online change-of-residence
procedure: the comune points you to the national **ANPR portal** first, with
paper and PEC as fallbacks.

- **Online:** log in to ANPR with **SPID, CIE** or **CNS** and submit the
  residence declaration for yourself and your household; the comune's *Sportello
  Telematico* hosts the same "cambio abitazione o residenza" service.
- **Paper / PEC:** alternatively, download the comune's residence-change form
  (plus a simplified version for minors and protected adults) and submit it on
  paper or by PEC to **protocollo@pec.comune.pescara.it**.
- **In person:** Ufficio Anagrafe, **Via Calabria 3** — Mon/Wed/Fri 8:30–12:00;
  Tue/Thu also 15:30–17:00. Demographic-services phone line **085 4283700**
  (residency is option 4): Mon/Wed/Fri 9:00–11:00, Tue/Thu 15:30–17:00.
- **Timeline:** national rule — the comune records the declaration (provisionally)
  within **2 days**, then an address-check phase of up to **45 days**. Put your
  surname on the **doorbell and mailbox**: the Polizia Locale uses it to verify.
- **Local quirks:** registration in ANPR auto-updates Motorizzazione, INPS and
  Agenzia delle Entrate; the comune expects you to regularise your **TARI**
  (waste-tax) position when you declare.
- **EU citizens:** registration is required for stays over **3 months** (proof of
  work, study or resources + health cover); once registered you can request the
  *attestazione di iscrizione anagrafica* online — €16 marca da bollo, issued
  within 30 days.
