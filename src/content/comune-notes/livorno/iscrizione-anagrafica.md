In **Livorno**, residency is handled by the **Ufficio Anagrafe e Notifiche**.

- **How to submit:** email residence declarations/changes to
  **cambiresidenza@comune.livorno.it**, or book an appointment through the
  comune's online booking system; general queries go to
  infoanagrafe@comune.livorno.it.
- **EU citizens:** registration follows Legislative Decree 30/2007; those resident
  for at least three months (for study, work, health or family) can also be
  entered in the temporary-population register for a set period.
- **Documents (EU citizens):** valid passport/ID, codice fiscale, and proof of the
  basis for your stay (work, study + cover, or sufficient resources + cover).
