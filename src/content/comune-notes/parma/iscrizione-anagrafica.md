In **Parma**, a first registration from abroad is filed **only through the
comune's online service**. You log in with SPID, CIE, CNS or an eIDAS identity,
or create a username and password tied to your email. With a username and
password, you download the finished declaration, sign it by hand and attach it
before sending. A delegate who holds SPID, CIE or CNS can file for you with a
signed *mandato di rappresentanza*.

- **Help in person:** book an appointment at the Direzionale Uffici Comunali
  (DUC), Largo Torello de Strada 11/a, on **0521 40521** — staff there help you
  submit the online declaration.
- **EU citizens:** with a recognised national eIDAS identity you can also file
  through the **ANPR** portal's service for European citizens. The comune then
  issues the *attestazione di regolarità di soggiorno*.
- **What to attach:** an ID card or passport for each adult, the cadastral
  details of the home, and your title to live there (deed, registered lease or
  registered free loan). A guest attaches the comune's Allegato 2, signed by the
  owner or current occupant, with a copy of their ID. EU citizens add proof of the
  residence conditions of D.Lgs. 30/2007.
- **Timeline:** the change is registered within **2 working days**, effective
  from the declaration date; the office checks the requirements within 45 days.
- **Afterwards:** activate your TARI (waste tax) account with Iren, as the comune
  reminds new residents.
