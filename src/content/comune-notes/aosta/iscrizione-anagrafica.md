In **Aosta**, residency paperwork runs mostly **in person and by appointment**:
the comune's registry desks see the public *solo su appuntamento*, while the
national **ANPR** portal covers the online side (certificates in real time with
SPID/CIE/CNS, and the national online residence declaration).

- **Where:** the *Ufficio Anagrafe* at the town hall (Piazza Émile Chanoux 1),
  by appointment only — tel. +39 0165 1875002, anagrafe@comune.aosta.it. Forms
  for residence changes are published via the regional CELVA shared-services
  portal linked from the office page.
- **EU citizens:** staying beyond three months you must apply for *iscrizione
  anagrafica*; Aosta takes the application at the *Amico in Comune* desk by
  appointment, with ID, work contract (or other basis for the stay), rental
  contract and, for dependants, a *presa in carico* declaration. Once
  registered you can request the *attestazione di iscrizione anagrafica*.
- **Timeline:** registration follows the national rule (completed within two
  working days), with address checks afterwards.
- **Local quirk:** Valle d'Aosta's administration is bilingual — the comune's
  site and services are offered in both Italian and French.
