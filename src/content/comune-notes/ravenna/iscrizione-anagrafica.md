In **Ravenna**, first registration from abroad (*nuova iscrizione in Anagrafe*)
splits by citizenship and digital identity.

- **EU citizens:** submit the residence declaration online on the national
  **ANPR** portal (anagrafenazionale.interno.it), logging in with the digital
  identity of your EU home country (**eIDAS**); the comune's registry staff then
  process it directly.
- **Non-EU citizens (or EU citizens without a digital identity):** file in
  person at the **Sportello polifunzionale, Viale Enrico Berlinguer 30**, by
  appointment only — call **0544 482482** (Mon–Sat 8:30–12:30) or book online
  (servizionline.comune.ra.it). Counter hours: Mon–Fri 8–13, Sat 8:30–12:30,
  plus Tue/Thu 14:30–16:30. Email: anagrafe@comune.ravenna.it.
- **Timeline:** the declaration is registered within **two working days**; the
  residency is confirmed within **45 days** after local-police address checks,
  unless you receive a pre-rejection notice by registered mail.
- **Local quirks:** the paper form must be signed by every adult in the move,
  with ID copies attached, and includes a housing-title section (DL 47/2014).
  Ravenna requires the apartment's **internal number** even though the
  ministerial form marks it optional, and recommends putting your name on the
  doorbell and letterbox before the address check.
