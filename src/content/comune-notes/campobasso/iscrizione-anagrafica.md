In **Campobasso**, the residency declaration is handled by the **Servizi
Demografici** in Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II, with the online route running
through the national **ANPR** portal.

- **How to submit:** online via ANPR with **SPID, CIE, CNS or eIDAS**; by
  **PEC** to `comune.campobasso.protocollo@pec.it` (attach the digitally signed
  declaration form); by registered mail (A/R) to *Comune di Campobasso, Servizi
  demografici, Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II, 86100 Campobasso*; or in person at
  the **Ufficio Protocollo**, Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II 29. The form is in the
  *Documenti* section of the service page; the procedure is free.
- **Timeline:** registration within **2 working days**; residency and family
  certificates from day 3, and the checks must close within **45 days** —
  silence counts as approval (*silenzio-assenso*).
- **EU citizens:** a dedicated ANPR flow ("Trasferimento della residenza in
  Italia") covers moves from abroad for stays over 3 months, including non-EU
  family members; have your ID/passport, codice fiscale and property or lease
  details ready, plus proof of your basis for staying.
- **Local quirk:** put every household member's name on the intercom, doorbell
  and mailbox — the municipal police verify presence at the address — and
  regularise local taxes (Via Cavour offices) within 60 days of registration.
