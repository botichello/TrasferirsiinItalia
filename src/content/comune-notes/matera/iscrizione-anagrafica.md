In **Matera**, the residence declaration goes to the **Ufficio Anagrafe** in the
Palazzo Municipale, **Viale Aldo Moro 32**, through one of three channels.

- **How to submit:** by **PEC** to `comune.matera@cert.ruparbasilicata.it`
  (completed declaration plus document copies); **in person** at the Anagrafe
  desk with a **mandatory appointment**, booked on the comune website or via the
  URP (tel. 0835 241312, ground floor of the Palazzo Municipale); or online via
  the national **ANPR** portal (SPID, CIE, CNS or eIDAS).
- **Documents:** Matera wants photocopies of everything — the signed declaration
  (allegato 1) for all adults moving, IDs, driving licences and vehicle
  registration papers, proof of title to the home (deed, lease or comodato), a
  copy of the electricity bill and codice fiscale, plus the receipt of the
  **TARI (waste tax) declaration** — a local quirk.
- **EU citizens:** must meet the residence conditions of D.lgs. 30/2007 (work,
  or sufficient resources + health cover); non-EU citizens show a permesso di
  soggiorno.
- **Timeline:** declare within 20 days of moving; Matera closes the procedure
  within **45 days**, after address checks. No cost.
