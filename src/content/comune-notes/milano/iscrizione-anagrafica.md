**Milano** runs residency registration largely **online**. EU and other foreign
citizens arriving from abroad use the dedicated *"Richiesta di residenza per
persone straniere provenienti dall'estero"* service, or **book an online
appointment** with a municipal officer for the first registration.

- **Access:** log in with **SPID, CIE** (electronic ID), **CNS**, or **eIDAS**
  (digital identity from another EU country).
- **Timeline:** registration is generally completed **within two working days**
  of submission; address checks follow afterwards.
- **Documents (EU citizens):** valid passport/ID, codice fiscale, and proof of the
  basis for your stay (work, study + cover, or sufficient resources + cover).
