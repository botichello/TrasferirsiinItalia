> **Draft — verify with your local AUSL before relying on it.**

In **Toscana**, the health service is delivered through three area authorities,
the **Aziende USL** (AUSL): Toscana Centro, Toscana Nord Ovest, and Toscana Sud
Est. You enrol with the one covering your registered address (in Firenze, this
is the **AUSL Toscana Centro**).

- **Where to enrol:** the AUSL covering your registered address; use the Regione
  Toscana health pages to find it and its counters (*sportelli*).
- **Choosing a doctor:** select a *medico di base* from those available in your
  AUSL district.
- **Economically inactive EU citizens:** confirm with your AUSL whether voluntary
  enrolment and the related annual contribution apply — this is set regionally,
  so the amount differs from other regions.
