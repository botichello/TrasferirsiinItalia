**Bolzano** is in bilingual South Tyrol (Italian/German). EU citizens resident for
at least three months request *iscrizione anagrafica* and the related attestation.

- **How to submit:** online via the comune's **SDO** portal or the national
  **ANPR** portal; or by PEC (a single signed PDF to 1.4.0@pec.bolzano.bozen.it)
  or registered mail. EU citizens attach the documentation in **Allegato B**.
- **Timeline:** registered within **2 working days** of receipt, with 45 days for
  verification.
- **Documents (EU citizens):** valid passport/ID, codice fiscale, and proof of the
  basis for your stay (work, study + cover, or sufficient resources + cover).
