In **Novara**, the residency declaration is handled by the **Anagrafe** at
**Via Fratelli Rosselli 1** and can be filed through several channels.

- **How to submit:** online via the national **ANPR** portal (SPID, CIE,
  CNS or eIDAS), via the comune's own portal (*unica.comune.novara.it*),
  **in person by appointment** (booked at *prenotazioni.comune.novara.it*),
  or by post (registered or ordinary mail) to *Ufficio Anagrafe, via
  Rosselli 1, 28100 Novara*. Email/PEC is **not** an accepted submission
  channel — PEC (*anagrafe@pec.comune.novara.it*) is for contact only.
- **Timeline:** preliminary registration within **2 working days** (after
  which you can request residence certificates); checks — including visits
  by the local police — are completed within **45 days**. The service is free.
- **Documents:** declaration form, codice fiscale and valid ID for every
  household member, proof of your right to occupy the property (cadastral
  data, lease or *comodato*), plus driving-licence and vehicle-plate data.
- **EU citizens:** must also meet the *Allegato B* requirements — proof of
  work, or study/sufficient resources plus health cover.
